# Reply-All to 214 People

Entry for **The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head** on make.fun: one line about office life, a real joke with a setup and a turn, punchline last.

> I hit reply-all to 214 people to ask to be removed from this thread, and 37 of them replied-all to ask the same thing.

The joke turns on the reply-all thread — something anyone who has worked in an office knows — and the mechanism is self-defeating: the request to leave the thread is itself a reply-all, and 37 people copy it.
